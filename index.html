How to play

Red (top screen)

W jump · S use ability

A D run left / right

Blue (bottom screen)

↑ jump · ↓ use ability

← → run left / right

Everyone races through the same course with a wall of fire behind them that keeps getting faster. Keep running right. If a danger gets you, you come back a little way behind a moment later; if the fire catches you, you're out. The last runner left wins.

Before you start

  • Players — the 1 2 3 4 buttons, top left. Green: T jump, F H run, G power. Yellow: I, J L, K. With 3 or 4 the race goes fullscreen.
  • Presets — Standard (every biome, secret ones found in special ways), Basic (the main biomes only) or Full (every biome, secret ones come up like any other). Picking one also resets the multipliers.
  • Mode — Normal, Wild (everything from the start, faster, and more of it) or Easy (no creatures or special attacks, and a slightly slower fire).
  • Biomes — turn them on, and pick which with the arrow. Locked ones are still being made.
  • Multipliers — your speed and jump (1x to 2x) and the fire's speed (0.5x to 1.5x).
  • Best distance (1 player) — one for each preset, in Normal and in Wild. Not saved in Easy, or with a multiplier changed.

The course

Spikes, pits, steps, floating, moving and crumbling platforms, swinging maces, saw blades, spike shooters and portals. Every danger warns you before it strikes, and there's always a way through. Each biome's own dangers start at 400 m.

Biomes

  • Prairie — missiles, bear traps (wiggle free) and bushes to hide in.
  • Taiga — slippery ice, falling icicles, rolling snowballs, thin ice over ponds and bears.
  • Desert — tall cactuses, quicksand (jump out), tumbleweeds, sinkholes and vultures.
  • Jungle — rivers with drifting logs, falling coconuts, flytraps (bait them first), arrow turrets, jaguars and panthers.

Orbs

  • Gold works at once: swap places, freeze, dash, and each biome's storm.
  • Light blue waits until you press your power key: wind dash, lightning, the mystic hand, the rescue drone, and each biome's own.
  • Purple only turns up when you've fallen far behind, once a race: portal gun, grapple or vine.

Secret worlds

  • Enchanted forest — fall asleep under the giant purple mushroom (once a race). The forest is out to get you: bouncy mushrooms, Mushroom Lobbers, living trees and Leave, the walking tree. Get out and you keep magic feet (bridges over every hole) and the roots (they grab the other player).
  • Arcane ruins — run over the button under the old stone portal. You have 20 seconds to reach the exit, past magic fire, lasers, electric slabs and the mortar. Make it and the aurora's lights shield you from a hit every 5 seconds for the rest of the race.
  • Military camp (still being made) — jump and grab the rope of the helicopter that flies in once a race. Watch the towers: soldiers who can't aim, grenades that bounce and roll at you, snipers (when the star in the window flashes, stop!), searchlights (hide under cover, or a truck full of soldiers comes for you) and tear gas (slow and blurry). Watch for landmines (a bump with a blinking red light): step on one and it clicks, so jump off fast or it throws you far back and the landing kills you. Jump on the crates there for bullets (wooden crates: everyone gets their own; steel ammo cans: first come, first served). At the end, the first one there flies home by jet with an M16 (twice your bullets, full auto), everyone else by helicopter with a pistol (it waits 5 seconds for anyone still in the camp, or leaves at once if there's nobody). Your power key shoots the way you face; a bullet kills. Out of bullets, press again to throw the gun: it stuns whoever it hits. In the middle of the camp there's a big red button under glass: smash it with your power key and a nuke falls on whoever's furthest ahead (nobody out there: on the camp itself). It can't be dodged: they're out for 3 seconds and radiated for the rest of the race, every so often going dizzy (blurry and slow). But then the helicopter home gets shot down. Alone, it nukes the fire back 30 m. Alone, the crates are gas cans: the jet sets you down far from the fire, further for each can.

1 player
